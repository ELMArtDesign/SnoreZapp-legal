Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy

Developer: ELMArt Design · App: SnoreZapp · Contact: elmartdesign@gmail.com · Last updated: November 19, 2026

This supplemental notice applies under the Washington My Health My Data Act, Nevada consumer health data laws, Connecticut Data Privacy Act, and similar US state laws.

  • Categories of consumer health data collected by ELMArt Design: None. Snoring estimates, optional evening diary entries, and exercise logs stay exclusively on your device (SnoreZapp has no Internet permission).
  • Sharing and sale: None. ELMArt Design does not collect, share, or sell consumer health data.
  • Third parties: None.
  • Retention & your rights (access, deletion, withdrawal of consent): Data stays on your phone until you delete it directly inside the app using "Delete everything" or by uninstalling the app, or you may contact elmartdesign@gmail.com.
